L7: # Updates on Tick Size and Step Size for Spot Trading Pairs
L8: 
L9: Published on 2021-08-12 15:57
L10: 
L11: Fellow Binancians,
L12: In order to increase market liquidity and improve trading experience, Binance will be adjusting the Tick Size (i.e., the minimum change in the unit price) and Step Size (i.e., the intervals that a quantity/iceberg quantity can be increased or decreased by) of certain spot trading pairs at 2021-08-26 06:00 AM (UTC). For details about all Tick Sizes and Step Sizes on Binance after this change, please refer to the cite2†Trading Rules .
L13:   * The adjustment will not affect spot trading and other relevant functions.
L14:   * Tick size and step size via API will also change. API users may use GET /api/v3/exchangeInfo for the latest tick size and step size. For further details and updates, please refer to our full cite3†API Changelog†github.com .
L15:   * The tick size and step size update will not affect existing spot orders. After the tick size and step size are updated, orders placed before the update will still be matched with the original tick size and step size.
L16: Please adjust your trading strategies accordingly to avoid unnecessary impact on your trading. We apologize for any inconvenience caused and thank you for your patience.
