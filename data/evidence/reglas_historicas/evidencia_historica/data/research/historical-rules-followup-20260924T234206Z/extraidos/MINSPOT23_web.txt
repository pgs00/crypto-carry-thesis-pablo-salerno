L11: Published on 2023-08-24 02:05

L13: Fellow Binancians,
L14: 
L15: In order to make trading more accessible and improve trading experience, Binance will reduce the minimum order size of spot trading pairs with DAI, USDT, TUSD, USDC as quote assets by 2023-08-31 03:00 (UTC), as well as spot trading pairs with ETH, BNB as quote assets by 2023-08-31 07:00 (UTC).
L16: For details about the minimum order sizes of all spot trading pairs on Binance, please refer to the cite9†Trading Rules .
L17: 
L18:   * The adjustment will not affect spot trading or other relevant functions.
L19: 
L20:   * API users may use GET /api/v3/exchangeInfo for the latest minimum order size. For further API details and updates, please refer to our cite10†API Changelog†github.com .
L21: 
L22:   * The updates to minimum order size will not affect existing spot orders.
L23:   * Where any discrepancy arises between the translated versions and the original English version, the English version shall prevail.
L24: 
L25: Details of the adjustments are shown in the table below:
L26: 
L27:   * By 2023-08-31 03:00 (UTC):
L28: 
L29: Trading Pairs With the Following Quote Assets  | Previous Minimum Order Size  | Updated Minimum Order Size
L30: DAI  | 10 DAI  | 5 DAI
L31: USDT  | 10 USDT  | 5 USDT
L32: TUSD  | 10 TUSD  | 5 TUSD
L33: USDC  | 10 USDC  | 5 USDC
L34: 
L35:   * By 2023-08-31 07:00 (UTC):
L36: Trading Pairs With the Following Quote Assets  | Previous Minimum Order Size  | Updated Minimum Order Size
L37: ETH  | 0.005 ETH  | 0.001 ETH
L38: BNB  | 0.05 BNB  | 0.01 BNB
