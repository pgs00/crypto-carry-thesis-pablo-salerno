L10: 
L11: Published on 2023-10-27 12:40
L12: 
L13: Fellow Binancians,
L14: 
L15: Binance Futures will update the minimum notional value for orders on BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT Perpetual Contracts by 2023-11-02 10:00 (UTC).
L16: 
L17: Details of the adjustments are shown in the table below:
L18: Symbol  | Previous Minimum Notional Value   | New Minimum Notional Value
L19: BTCUSDT Perpetual Contract  | 5.0 USDT  | 100.0 USDT
L20: ETHUSDT Perpetual Contract  | 5.0 USDT  | 20.0 USDT
L21: 
L22: Please Note:
L23: 
L24:   * The aforementioned updates will not impact existing orders on BTCUSDT and ETHUSDT Perpetual Contracts.
L25:   * Futures Grid Trading Bot orders for the aforementioned contracts will automatically expire if they do not meet the corresponding minimum notional value requirement. Users are strongly advised to update their Futures Grid Trading Bots prior to 2023-11-02 10:00 (UTC).
L26: 
L27:   * API users could use GET /fapi/v1/exchangeInfo to check the latest minimum notional values for orders on each symbol. For further API details and updates, please refer to our cite9†API Change Log†binance-docs.github.io .
