L10: 
L11: Published on 2022-07-06 14:00

L15: Fellow Binancians,
L16: To celebrate Binance’s fifth anniversary, Binance will introduce zero-fee trading for BTC spot trading pairs at 2022-07-08 14:00 (UTC). The zero-fee trading will cover the following 13 spot trading pairs: BTC/AUD, BTC/BIDR, BTC/BRL, BTC/BUSD, BTC/EUR, BTC/GBP, BTC/RUB, BTC/TRY, BTC/TUSD, BTC/UAH, BTC/USDC, BTC/USDP and BTC/USDT.
L17: 
L18: Validity Period: 2022-07-08 14:00 (UTC) until further notice
L19:   * All users will enjoy zero maker and taker fees for all 13 BTC spot trading pairs.
L20:   * The 13 BTC spot trading pairs will be excluded from BNB fee discounts, fee rebates or any other form of fee adjustments or fee promotions.

L23:   * Binance reserves the right to disqualify trades that are deemed to be wash trades or illegally bulk registered accounts, as well as trades that display attributes of self-dealing or market manipulation.
